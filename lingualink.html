Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

LinguaLink ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application for real-time voice translation between foreign languages and Vietnamese. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

In short: we do not collect, store, or sell any of your data. The app has no backend server of its own. Your voice is sent directly from your device to the AI provider you choose, using your own API key, and your conversation history stays on your device.

1. Information the App Processes

1.1 Voice Audio

When you start a translation session, audio from your microphone is streamed directly from your device to the AI translation provider you selected (Google Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic Claude), using your own API key. We never receive, store, or have access to your audio. Audio is processed by the provider in real time to produce translations; their handling of this data is governed by their own privacy policies (see Section 4).

1.2 Text You Translate

Text entered in the text-translation screen is sent directly to the AI provider (Anthropic Claude) with your own API key, in the same way as voice audio.

1.3 API Keys

You provide your own API keys for the AI providers. Keys are stored only on your device, in encrypted secure storage (Android Keystore). They are transmitted only to the corresponding provider's official API endpoint to authenticate your requests.

1.4 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Conversation history: transcripts and translations of your sessions, stored in a local database. You can delete individual sessions or all history in the app at any time.
  • Custom glossary: term pairs you define, stored locally.
  • Usage statistics: estimated API usage/cost figures, computed and stored locally.
  • Settings: display language and preferences, stored locally.

None of this data leaves your device, except when you explicitly use the "Share transcript" feature, which passes the text to the app you choose via the Android share sheet.

1.5 Device Permissions

  • Microphone: required to capture your voice for translation. Audio is only captured during an active translation session.
  • Notifications: to show a persistent notification while a translation session runs in the background.
  • Display over other apps (optional): to show a small floating bubble with live translation when you minimize the app during a session. You can deny this permission; the app works without it.

2. Information We Collect

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and we operate no server that receives your data.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All connections to AI providers use encrypted transport (HTTPS/WSS).
  • API keys are stored in encrypted secure storage on your device.
  • Conversation history, glossary, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data.

4. Third-Party Services

Depending on the provider you choose, your audio/text is processed by:

  • Google Gemini API — Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI API — OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Claude API — Anthropic Privacy Policy

You use these services under your own account and API key; we are not a party to that relationship.

5. Data Retention

  • We retain nothing, because we collect nothing.
  • Local data (history, glossary, keys, settings) remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Retention of data by AI providers is governed by their own policies.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete any or all conversation history in the app
  • Remove your API keys in the app's Settings
  • Revoke device permissions through your phone's Settings
  • Uninstall the app to erase all local data

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
